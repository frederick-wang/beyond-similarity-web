# Chinese is a full counterpart at `/zh/`, not a summary

English lives at `/` and Chinese at `/zh/`, with the same sections, figures, tables, and numbers, linked through hreflang. Scholars of classical Chinese and Chinese-language AI assistants search in Chinese; a summary would leave out the evidence they come for and would be quoted as a lesser source than the English page.

## Considered Options

- A Chinese summary block on the English page. Rejected for the reason above.
- A separate Chinese subdomain. Rejected: a path keeps both languages in one site, one sitemap, and one deployment.
