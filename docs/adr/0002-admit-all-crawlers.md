# robots.txt admits every crawler, including AI training crawlers

The paper page exists to be found and quoted accurately by search engines and generative AI. `robots.txt` therefore admits every crawler, including those that collect model-training data (such as GPTBot, Google-Extended, and CCBot): appearing in training corpora is one of the ways models learn about the paper, and the page holds nothing the authors need to withhold from them.

## Considered Options

- Admit search crawlers only and block training crawlers. Rejected: it gives up reach that the page is built for, in exchange for control over public, abstract-level material.
